# Privacy Policy

This extension respects your privacy.

- **No Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or usage data.
- **Local Storage**: All your tasks and settings are stored locally on your device using `chrome.storage.local`.
- **External Checkout Link**: The extension does not send your tasks or settings to external servers. If you click the upgrade link, your browser opens an external Stripe Checkout page, and any payment information entered there is handled by Stripe.
